# Code of Conduct

RepoInsight follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and give newcomers the patience you would have wanted.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else
  connected to the project.

If something goes wrong, contact the maintainer privately through
[github.com/gativarshney](https://github.com/gativarshney). Reports are kept confidential.
The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts that break these
rules.
